description: Ask the captain for a new joke, found on the web by his crew.
---

Send minino-buscachistes to look for new jokes on the web and pick the one you like best. If the parrot is aboard (jokebook:loro-critico, which comes with your jokebook), pass it the joke first: if it fails it, ask for another batch, only once. Tell the joke in character and mention in passing where the kitten found it. Note it in your logbook as a `joke` page with the URL and, if the parrot scored it, its score.
